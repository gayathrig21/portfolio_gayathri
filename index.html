Earlier roles at Cognizant and iGate gave me a foundation in data engineering, ETL, data warehousing, Java applications, and reporting. Across these roles, I’ve also mentored engineers, led root-cause investigations, and worked with international stakeholders.

I’ve worked with Oracle Fusion AI Studio on payroll readiness and forecasting workflows. Alongside that professional experience, I’m new to independent LLM application development and am learning through small projects, including an email assistant that drafts replies in the tone of the incoming message.
